### Where Your Data is Stored

By using our services, you acknowledge and consent that your personal data, including your name, surname, mobile number, and email address, may be stored and processed on secure servers located outside South Africa, specifically in the European Union (EU). The EU has strong data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which provides safeguards for your personal information.

### Why We May Store Data Internationally

We may store data outside South Africa to enhance the performance, reliability, and security of our services. Storing data in the EU ensures compliance with international data protection standards and provides a high level of security for your personal information.

### Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your data. These measures include encryption, restricted access, and regular security audits.

### Use of Donation Credits

Our services operate using Donation Credits, a form of donation balance that users can fund into their account. Once funded, Donation Credits belong solely to Qalb as a registered nonprofit organization (NPO). Users can specify to which Participating Mosques the credits should be allocated, but cannot send, transfer, redeem for cash, or otherwise control the credits themselves.
