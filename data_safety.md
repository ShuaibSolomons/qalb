# DATA SAFETY

Last updated: November 17, 2025

## Welcome to QALB

This Data Safety outlines the rules and regulations for the use of QALB's services.

By utilising our services you accept this data safety policy. Do not continue to use QALB if you do not agree to all of the terms stated on this page.

At Qalb, your privacy, security, and control over your data are our top priorities. This page explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

---

### Information We Collect

#### Account Information:
- Name, email, mobile number

#### Transaction Information:
- Donation Credits funded and allocated to Participating Mosques
- Payment history (processed securely via Yoco or other payment gateways)

#### Device & Usage Information
- IP address, device type, operating system, browser/app usage patterns

#### Analytics
- Data used to monitor app performance, improve user experience, and track anonymized usage






### How We Use Your Data

- To provide and maintain Qalb services
- To process Donation Credit allocations securely
- To communicate updates, notifications, and support responses
- To improve app performance and functionality
- To comply with legal obligations, including donation reporting




### Data Storage & International Transfer
- Personal data may be stored and processed on secure servers in the European Union (EU)
- Storing data in the EU ensures compliance with international data protection standards (GDPR)
- By using Qalb, you consent to the storage and processing of your data outside South Africa
- Users can revoke consent at any time by contacting support@qalb.co.za





### Data Sharing

- Only with third-party service providers required to deliver our services (e.g., payment processors, analytics tools)
- Legal or regulatory authorities if required by law
- Qalb does not sell your data





### Security Measures
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
- Security audits and best practices ensure ongoing protection
- Users are responsible for safeguarding their account credentials


### User Rights
- Access & Correction: Users can view or update their personal information
- Account Deletion: Users can delete their account by contacting support@qalb.co.za
- Revoking Consent: Users can revoke consent for data storage or processing at any time



### Important Notes

- All Donation Credits remain owned by Qalb and cannot be cashed out or transferred outside the platform
- Transactions are final once allocated
- Continued use of Qalb constitutes consent to this Data Safety and Privacy Policy
